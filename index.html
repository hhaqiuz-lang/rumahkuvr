About the Project

What is RumahKuVR?

RumahKuVR helps senior citizens practise identifying and resolving household hazards without facing real danger. Users explore a virtual Malaysian home, interact with safety tools and household objects, complete corrective actions, receive scores, and review their performance after each training session.

✓

Safe Training

Practise potentially risky household situations inside a controlled virtual environment.

↻

Interactive Learning

Learn through hands-on corrective actions rather than passive reading or simple quiz questions.

◎

Personalised Guidance

Receive recommendations based on safety performance, mistakes, hint usage, and previous sessions.

Interactive Hazard Training

Learning by taking the correct safety action

Easy Level scenarios focus on recognising danger, selecting appropriate tools, following a safe sequence, and observing environmental feedback.

Wet Floor

The user identifies a slipping hazard and physically completes a safe corrective sequence.

Exposed Electrical Wire

AI-Assisted Guidance

Personalised Safety Guidance

RumahKuVR uses an AI-assisted rule-based expert system to analyse gameplay information such as safety scores, missed hazards, incorrect action sequences, completion time, hint usage, penalties, and previous session performance.

The predefined home-safety rules are used to identify areas that require improvement, recommend repeated hazard training, adjust guidance levels, suggest suitable difficulty levels, and provide personalised recommendations to senior citizens and caregivers.

Technical scope: The current design is rule-based. It does not claim to use machine learning, generative AI, computer vision, or large language models.
